Welcome to **Gânduri Intruzive** (Intrusive Thoughts).

This blog is a personal journal of reflections, philosophical musings, and fragments of thought written across different moments in time. Each post represents a snapshot of ideas exploring themes of identity, existence, freedom, and the human condition.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Philosophical reflections** on life, death, meaning, and consciousness
- **Artistic explorations** of language, beauty, and the nature of being
- **Personal essays** written with honesty and depth
- **Meditations** on freedom, sacrifice, and moral law

## About the Author

These thoughts are written by **Andrei**, shared as a way of processing the complexities of existence and contributing to ongoing conversations about what it means to be human.

## A Note on These Writings

The posts here are not meant to be complete or definitive. They are fragments—pieces of reflection offered without pretension to absolute truth. If something resonates with you, explore it further. If something challenges you, sit with that discomfort.
